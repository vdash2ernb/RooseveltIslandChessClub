Questions parents ask

Good questions.

My child has never played. Is that OK?

Yes. Many students start by learning how the pieces move, and our beginner groups start from the very first move.

How do you choose my child's group?

Coaches group students by age, experience and chess level whenever possible, so every child is challenged. The trial class is where we work this out together.

What happens in the $30 trial class?

Your child joins a class. A coach gets to know them, checks their current level and recommends the best group. Then you decide.

What do the classes cover?

Fundamentals and good habits, tactics and pattern recognition, opening principles, middlegame strategy, checkmates and endgames, puzzles and calculation, practice games, game analysis, and tournament preparation for students who want to compete.

Are there tournaments?

Yes. The Sunday semester includes a practice tournament, the club runs blitz events and workshops, and coaches can point families to outside tournaments that suit their child's level.

How long is a class, and what does it cost?

Every class is 60 minutes. Weekly classes are $175 a month. The Sunday beginners semester is $350 for 10 classes. The trial class is $30.
